# Read aloud and translate sentences, paragraphs and selections (Alt, Alt+Shift, selection bar)

Modeled on echo-read-edge's modifier-selection. Works in both the Bot view and the worker chat.

|Gesture|Acts on|Does|
|---|---|---|
|Hold Alt|the sentence under the pointer|highlights it|
|Hold Alt+Shift|the paragraph under the pointer|highlights it; letting Shift go (Alt still held) goes back to the sentence|
|Alt+click / Alt+Shift+click|sentence / paragraph|reads it aloud; it becomes the region|
|Alt+right-click / Alt+Shift+right-click|sentence / paragraph|translates it; it becomes the region|
|Drag-select with the left button|the selection|a bar at the pointer: **Read aloud**, **Translate**|
|Click on the region|the region|while it is read: pauses; click again resumes where it paused. Once nothing reads it: reads it again|
|Double-click a word in the region|the region|reads on (or again) from that word's sentence, from about that word|
|Right-click on the region|the region|translates it|
|Press elsewhere, or Escape|the region|stops its read and forgets it|

Shift alone is never a gesture: Shift+click extends a selection. What an Alt gesture picks, or Read aloud on a selection reads, becomes the region: shaded until a press elsewhere, Escape, another pick, or its text going away. While it is read aloud, its sentences are read one after another and the one being read is highlighted, moving on as each is heard (the read-along).

## Settings

- `voiceAgent.messageButtons` (default off): Settings → Voice → "Voice agent" → "Read aloud and translate sentences (Alt)". Messages have no buttons.
- When on, a "Translate into" dropdown sets `voiceAgent.translateTo`: 20 languages from `src/shared/translationLanguages.ts`, Google codes, default `zh-CN`.
- `voiceAgent.replayCacheSize`: speech cache size (default 30 messages/paragraphs).

## Webview side

- The host `VoicePanel` (`src/voiceAgent/voicePanel.ts`) sends `sentenceActions` to the whole webview whether or not the Bot view is shown: the toggle, the target language, and the sentence being read (`VoiceReplay`).
- `src/webview/sentenceActions.ts` stores it, sends `replay` / `translate`, and plays audio with Web Audio when voice mode is off (`replayAudio` / `replayClipStarted` / `replayClipEnded`). What is being read is `src/webview/readAlong.ts` (below).
- `src/webview/sentencePick.ts` is the single gesture controller: document-level, capture phase, it intercepts Alt+click / Alt+right-click (with or without Shift) so the page's own click handlers never see them (Alt+Shift+press would otherwise extend a selection). With the toggle off, Alt is not intercepted. While Alt is held it highlights the sentence under the pointer with `::highlight(vp-sentence-hover)` (no DOM changes); with Shift too (tracked by `keydown`/`keyup` and each `mousemove`'s `shiftKey`), the paragraph.
- Where sentences are is decided by each "surface" (`SentenceSurface.pick(node, offset, scope)` / `rangeOf`; `scope` is `sentence` or `paragraph`):
  - **Bot view** — `botSentences` in `src/webview/voicePanel.ts`: plain-text messages are split with `replayPieces` from `sentences.ts`, distinguished by `data-mode` on `.vp-body`; replies the voice agent spoke use one span per entry in their `sentences`; system lines are excluded. A paragraph is the message's text up to a blank line; a spoken reply is one paragraph whose `parts` are its spans' texts (exactly the pieces voice mode spoke and cached), not a re-split.
  - **Chat** — `chatSentences` in `src/webview/chat/sentenceSurface.ts`: text in `.message-content` (prompts, replies, summaries), `.thinking-content` (Thought), and tool card `.tv-out` (e.g. a task's assignment), split per containing paragraph (p, li, headings, table cells, card text-block pre, etc.). A paragraph is that block's own text (a nested list is its own), cut at blank lines (a card's text block). Code blocks and buttons are excluded. The chat rebuilds history on every stateSync, so sentences are re-found by paragraph text; a `MutationObserver` triggers the re-find while a highlight is active.
- `pickedOf` in `sentencePick.ts` makes what a gesture picks from sentences (`VoiceReplayPiece`s with ranges): one sentence, or several read as one (a paragraph from `paragraphPieces`, a selection): then `piece.parts` are its sentences as `replayPieces` cuts them (the same texts Alt+click on each sentence sends, so the same cache entries), `piece.text` is them joined, `piece.range` runs from the first sentence's start to the last one's end, and `PickedSentence.parts` are the sentences themselves, each found by its surface's `rangeOf` (the read-along highlights them). Same `entryId` as the paragraph's sentences, so they share one cache slot.
- `SentenceSurface.holds(node)` says whether a node is in the surface's text; the selection bar uses it.
- Highlight, popup, selection bar and read-along styles are in `src/webview/styles/chat/sentences.css`.

## The region and the read-along (`src/webview/readAlong.ts`)

After echo-read-edge's click-to-listen (`src/content/modules/click-to-listen.ts`), adapted:

- **The region.** `ReadAlong.start` (a read sent: Alt+click, Read aloud, a click on the region) or `select` (Alt+right-click: picked, not read; a read of other text stops) makes the text the region. Unlike EchoRead, it outlives its read: played through, stopped or failed, it stays the control, and a click reads it again. It is forgotten on a press outside it, Escape, the next pick, the gestures being turned off (`clear`, which also stops its read), or when its text is no longer shown and nothing reads it.
- **Highlights** (CSS Custom Highlights): `vp-read-region` shades the region (the control; priority −1, under the others); while it is read, the sentence being read (`VoiceReplay.part`, index into `PickedSentence.parts`) is `vp-sentence-loading` (loading/queued), `vp-sentence-playing`, or `vp-sentence-paused`. The host moves `part` as each sentence is heard to start. Repainted on the `MutationObserver` redraw pass (which runs while a region is shown), so a chat redraw re-finds the ranges.
- The host reads the region when `sentenceActions.replay` matches it (entry id, text, sentence, range); a read sent but not yet reported survives the previous read's end.
- **Click vs double-click** (`ClickGate`, pure, tested): EchoRead's window and slop (`DOUBLE_CLICK_MS` 350, `CLICK_SLOP_PX` 4). Unlike EchoRead, which toggles on the first click at once, a click acts only once the window has passed with no second press near it, so a double-click never pauses first. The second press of a double-click is `preventDefault`ed, so the browser selects no word. A click elsewhere while one waits makes the waiting one act at once; a read ending drops a waiting click (it was meant to pause, not to read again). A press that ends more than 4 px away is a drag.
- **Click** (left button, no modifier, on the region's text: its line boxes ±4 px vertically, ±2 px horizontally) sends `replayControl` `pause`, or `resume` when paused; once nothing reads the region, `replay` of it again. The click is `preventDefault`ed and stopped (captured), so links and cards under it do not act. As in EchoRead, a press made with a selection present, or a click that leaves a selection (a drag inside the region), is not a click: text there can still be selected.
- **Double-click** finds the caret under the pointer (`caretRangeFromPoint`), measures characters from the region's start to it and to each sentence's bounds (`Range.toString`), and `seekPoint` (pure, tested) picks the sentence and the fraction of it where the word starts (a word backs up over letters, digits, `'’_-`; a Chinese/Japanese character is a word of its own; between sentences: the next one from its start). While the region is read, sends `replaySeek { part, fraction }`; otherwise `replay` of it with `from: { part, fraction }` (`ReplayPlayer.toggle` starts there, making nothing before it). EchoRead seeks to the word's TTS timestamp; our TTS returns no word timings, so the place is estimated by characters.
- **Right-click** (no modifier, on the region's text, no selection made) opens the translation panel for the region instead of the context menu (captured, `preventDefault`ed and stopped). Alt+right-click stays `sentencePick.ts`'s.
- **Stop**: a press outside the region's bounding box (not on a scrollbar, the translation panel or the selection bar), or Escape, sends `replayControl` `stop` (when it is read) and clears the highlights. (EchoRead stops only from its floating controller.) Alt gestures are left to `sentencePick.ts`; Alt+click on another sentence starts a new read, which replaces this one.
- While the pointer is on the region's text, the body gets `data-read-along` (pointer cursor).
- A selection read clears the browser's selection when it starts (as EchoRead), so the region shows instead. Translate on the selection bar does not make a region: the browser's selection stays.

## Selection bar (drag-select)

- `src/webview/selectionToolbar.ts`. On a left-button `mouseup` (not with Alt) it reads the selection on the next task (a click inside a selection clears it only then). The bar shows when the feature is on (`sentenceActions.enabled`), the selection is non-empty text, and `surfaceOfSelection` finds one surface holding both the first and the last text the selection covers (text only touched at an edge, as after a triple-click, does not count). A selection across two chat messages counts; one reaching into the composer or a header does not.
- The bar floats (`position: fixed`) centred above the `mouseup` point, below it when there is no room, kept inside the window (`toolbarPosition`). Pressing its buttons does not clear the selection (`mousedown` is prevented).
- **Read aloud** sends `replay` with `surface: 'selection'` and `entryId` `selection\u0000<text>`. The spoken text is `selectedText(range)`: the selection's text nodes as they are, a line break between blocks (so a heading without a full stop ends a sentence), code blocks and buttons left out; its sentences (`replayPieces`) are the read's parts, and each part's DOM range is kept, so the read-along highlights them. The button is disabled when nothing in the selection is spoken (only code or symbols).
- **Translate** opens the translation panel by the selection, pinned on its range.
- The selection's range is kept (as a `SentenceView` named `selection`) only while its text is still there; a redraw that replaces it (a chat stateSync) ends the highlight and closes the panel, as it drops the selection.
- Hidden on a press elsewhere, Escape, any scroll, a resize, the selection being cleared or changed, and the toggle being turned off.

## Read aloud (Alt+click, Alt+Shift+click, Read aloud)

- Sends `replay` (`VoiceReplayPiece` with a `range` or `sentence` index, `surface: bot|chat|selection`). For chat, `entryId` is `chat\u0000<paragraph text>`; for a selection, `selection\u0000<selected text>`; the host checks neither (only `bot` entries must still be shown).
- `ReplayPlayer.toggle` in `src/voiceAgent/replay.ts` reads the piece with the current TTS config (`resolveTtsConfig`). A paragraph or selection is read part by part (`piece.parts`): each part is looked up in the cache (hit/miss logged per part), only misses go to TTS (one request at a time, in order, and not stopped by a pause, so later sentences keep being made) and are `add`ed to the entry, and each clip is queued on the output as soon as it is ready, so the first sentence plays while later misses are synthesized. TTS config is only resolved if some part misses. Stopping (or the output taking the speakers back) aborts the pending TTS request and drops queued clips. Clicking the same sentence again stops; another sentence replaces it.
- `VoiceReplay.phase` goes loading → queued → playing, driven by real playback reports (the hidden audio page's `started`, or the webview Web Audio's `replayClipStarted`); each clip's start report also sets `part`, the sentence being read.
- **Start point** (`replay.from`, `ReplayPlayer.toggle(entryId, piece, from)`): a double-click on a region nothing reads starts the read at `fraction` into sentence `part`, as a seek would; the sentences before it are not made.
- **Pause / resume / seek** (`replayControl`, `replaySeek`; `ReplayPlayer.pause` / `resume` / `seek`). A read runs in "runs": each takes the speakers (`output()`), queues clips from a start point, and ends at the end of the read, a pause, or a seek. **Pause** ends the run's output (drops what is queued there, releases the speakers: voice mode hears the microphone again, dictation resumes) but keeps the read: every sentence's audio, and where the pause was heard: the playing clip's start report time plus the time since, minus 0.15 s so no syllable is lost (`RESUME_OVERLAP_SECS`); past the clip's end, the next sentence's start. Phase `paused`. **Resume** takes the speakers again and queues the rest of that sentence's audio (sliced PCM), then the next sentences. **Seek** does the same from `fraction` × the sentence's samples, less 0.2 s (`SEEK_LEAD_SECS`), whether playing or paused; a sentence still with TTS is waited for. The speakers are released while paused (rather than the audio page's AudioContext being suspended) so voice mode's mic hold and echo filter never stay on for a read nobody hears, and the page shared with the voice agent's speech is never left suspended. The voice agent speaking during a resume refuses it (the read ends with its error toast).
- **Cache.** Audio is first looked up in `SpeechCache` (`src/voiceAgent/speechCache.ts`): in-memory FIFO keyed by `entryId` + `ttsCacheKey` (engine/url/model/voice/speed); `piece()` fetches by sentence text, from the entry itself first, else from any other entry made with the same voice (same words, same audio: a selection, or chat text quoting a spoken reply). A voice-mode reply whose every sentence synthesized successfully is `put` whole through `VoiceMode`'s `onSpoken` (`Speaker.takeSpoken`). On a miss TTS is called and the result is `add`ed to the same entry (all sentences of one message take one slot).
- Failures show a toast via `replayError`.
- **Voice mode on:** played in the hidden audio page via `VoiceMode.beginReplay` (Chrome AEC cancels it). Mic input is dropped during playback and for 300 ms after; speech within 3 s that overlaps the replayed text is dropped as echo (`echoFilter.isEchoOf`, also used as echo reference for barge-in). The voice agent starting to speak, hush, standby, or stop takes the speaker back.
- **Voice mode off:** the webview plays with Web Audio (`BotViewAudio`, `replayAudio` / `replayClipEnded`); dictation (`DictationSession.setPaused`) discards recorded audio meanwhile, except while the read is paused.
- **Talking avatar:** either way, `ReplayPlayer` meters each clip from its start report (`ClipLevelMeter`, `src/voiceAgent/botLevel.ts`) and posts the level as `voiceLevel` from `bot`, so the voice agent's avatar talks as for a reply: in the Bot view the avatar of the reply being read (else the latest reply's), elsewhere the voice bar's (docs/voice-agent.md, Names and avatars). A pause or stop reports silence.

## Translate (Alt+right-click, Alt+Shift+right-click, Translate)

- Sends `translate` with a request id and target language `to`. `Translator` in `src/voiceAgent/googleTranslate.ts` calls Google `translate_a/single` (client=`dict-chrome-ex`) and replies with `translation`.
- Code spans are replaced by `⟪n⟫` placeholders and not sent for translation; if placeholders are lost, segments are translated one by one.
- If Google detects the text is already in the target language (for regional codes like zh-CN / zh-TW the region must also match), it replies `alreadyInTarget`.
- After a 429, requests pause per Retry-After.
- `src/webview/translatePopup.ts` shows a floating panel below the sentence, paragraph or selection (above if there is no room; `position: fixed`, clamped to its scroll container; title shows the target language), with an arrow pointing to the text's end; the text stays highlighted with `vp-sentence-pinned`. The panel follows the text on scroll and redraw, closes when the text is gone or the toggle is turned off, and on Esc, outside click, or ×. Translations are cached per language + text (200 entries).
